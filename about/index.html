Local knowledge for smoother travel

Dominican Quest is a Dominican Republic–based transportation and excursion company serving international visitors, local travelers, families, couples, wedding groups, and travel partners. Our work combines destination knowledge with practical coordination before, during, and after a reservation.

For more than 20 years, the team has helped travelers move between airports, hotels, resorts, villas, cities, ports, and activity locations across the country. The goal is straightforward: clear arrangements, suitable vehicles, professional drivers, and support when travel plans change.

What we arrange

  • Private one-way and round-trip airport transfers.
  • Hotel, villa, city, port, and inter-region transportation.
  • Wedding and group arrival coordination.
  • Excursions and customized travel activities.

What travelers can expect

Reservations can include flight details, passenger count, luggage needs, child-seat requests, pickup instructions, and the exact destination. Our team uses those details to coordinate the appropriate service and remain reachable through WhatsApp and email.
