A simpler arrival from airport to hotel

Dominican Quest provides prearranged private airport transportation across the Dominican Republic. Instead of negotiating transportation after landing, you can choose your route and group size before the trip, receive a suitable vehicle option, and keep your reservation details together.

Our service is designed for individual travelers, couples, families, and groups. Airport pickups can include your airline and flight number so the operations team has the information needed to coordinate your arrival. Return transfers can also be scheduled around your departure flight.

What to have ready

  • Your arrival airport and destination hotel, resort, villa, or address.
  • Your travel date, flight number, and expected arrival time.
  • The total number of passengers, including children.
  • Any special requirements such as child seats, extra luggage, or accessibility needs.

Private service for the route you actually need

The Dominican Republic has several international airports serving different resort regions. Selecting the airport closest to your destination can reduce unnecessary driving, but the best route depends on your flight availability and itinerary. Our airport guides below explain the main areas commonly served from each gateway.

Airport transfer questions

What happens if my arrival flight is delayed?

Include accurate airline and flight information with the reservation. The operations team uses those details to coordinate the airport pickup when the flight schedule changes.

Can I book a round trip?

Yes. Choose the round-trip option in the booking form and provide the return date and departure-flight details.

Can I request child seats or extra luggage space?

Add the request in the booking notes. Include the children’s ages and describe unusual luggage so the team can confirm the appropriate setup.
