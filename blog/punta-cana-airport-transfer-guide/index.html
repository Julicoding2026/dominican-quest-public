Punta Cana Airport Transfer Guide

Everything worth arranging before you land—from choosing the correct resort zone to meeting your driver and planning the return journey.

By the Dominican Quest teamUpdated August 18, 20267 min read

Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) is the main air gateway for Punta Cana and the east-coast resort region. The arrival itself is only one part of the plan: your hotel’s exact zone, your flight details, and the kind of transportation you reserve determine how easy the journey feels after baggage claim.

First, identify your resort zone

“Punta Cana” is often used for a much larger hotel region. Cap Cana and the original Punta Cana area sit closer to the airport, while Bávaro, El Cortecito, Arena Gorda, Macao, and Uvero Alto extend north along the coast. Two hotels marketed as Punta Cana can therefore have very different transfer times.

The Dominican Republic’s official tourism site gives useful orientation estimates from PUJ: about 15 minutes to Punta Cana and Cap Cana, 30 minutes to Bávaro, El Cortecito, and Arena Gorda, and 45 minutes to Macao and Uvero Alto. Treat those as planning estimates, not guarantees—traffic, weather, road work, terminal activity, and the exact hotel entrance can change the drive.

Use the full hotel name and address when reserving. Resort brands sometimes operate several properties with similar names.

What to provide when you book

A transfer company needs enough detail to follow the flight and assign the correct vehicle. Prepare:

  • Your arrival date, airline, and flight number.
  • The airport code PUJ, especially if your itinerary includes more than one Dominican airport.
  • The exact hotel, villa, or accommodation name and location.
  • The number of adults and children, plus the amount of luggage.
  • Any child-seat, accessibility, oversized-luggage, or group requirements.

If the booking covers your departure too, add the outbound flight number and scheduled departure time. A round trip is easier to coordinate when both journeys are attached to the same reservation.

Private transfer or another option?

A private transfer is reserved for your party and travels to your accommodation without unrelated hotel stops. That makes it especially practical after a long flight, for families, and for groups carrying several bags. Shared transportation can cost less for one traveler, but it normally follows a multi-stop route and fixed operating plan. Rental cars offer independence but also require navigating, parking, and understanding local driving conditions immediately after arrival.

Compare more than the headline price. Confirm whether the quoted rate is per passenger or per vehicle, what luggage capacity is included, whether the trip is direct, and how support works if you cannot locate the meeting point.

What happens on arrival

After your flight, complete the airport’s required arrival steps and collect every checked bag before heading to the agreed meeting area. Keep your confirmation and support contact accessible without relying only on airport Wi-Fi. If baggage is delayed or you are held inside the terminal, contact the transfer team as soon as you can.

Do not accept a ride from someone who cannot identify your reservation or the agreed company. Match the representative’s details with the instructions in your confirmation before leaving the pickup area.

Planning the return to PUJ

A departure pickup needs more margin than the drive time alone. Work backward from the airline’s recommended airport arrival time, then add the estimated road journey and a sensible traffic buffer. Your pickup time should be based on the terminal departure—not simply on hotel checkout.

Reconfirm the pickup location and time before travel day, especially at large resorts with multiple lobbies, gates, or guest areas. Be ready in the designated lobby with all bags before the scheduled time so the vehicle can leave promptly.

A simple arrival checklist

  • Verify that your flight lands at PUJ.
  • Book using the accommodation’s complete name.
  • Share the correct flight number and passenger count.
  • Request special equipment before confirmation.
  • Save your meeting instructions and support contact offline.
  • Confirm your return pickup before departure day.

Sources and further planning

Destination orientation and indicative resort-zone drive times are based on the Dominican Republic Ministry of Tourism’s air travel guide. Always use your airline, accommodation, and booking confirmation for trip-specific instructions.
