Direct private service from LRM

La Romana International Airport is a convenient gateway for stays around La Romana, Casa de Campo, Bayahibe, and Dominicus. Prearranging a private transfer lets the group move directly toward the accommodation with the reservation, destination, and passenger details established before arrival.

Dominican Quest offers private service on supported routes from LRM. Enter the exact hotel, villa, resort, or mapped location in the transfer search. If a destination does not appear, contact the reservations team with the complete itinerary so they can check whether a custom arrangement is possible.

Casa de Campo and La Romana

For gated properties and private villas, provide the precise property or meeting location and keep any access instructions available. Share the lead passenger’s reachable phone number in case coordination is needed at the entrance or reception.

Bayahibe and Dominicus

Bayahibe and Dominicus are popular bases for beach stays and island excursions. Airport transportation can be booked separately from tour activities, making it easier to organize arrival, departure, and excursion days around the same travel party.

Before the pickup

Confirm the airport code, airline, flight number, arrival time, passenger count, and luggage requirements. Travelers using sports equipment or carrying oversized bags should describe them in advance. For the return ride, provide the scheduled flight departure time and follow the confirmed pickup plan.
