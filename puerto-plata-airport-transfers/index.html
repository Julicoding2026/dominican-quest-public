Private arrival service for the north coast

Gregorio Luperón International Airport is a key gateway for Puerto Plata and nearby north-coast communities. A private transfer gives your party a direct plan from the airport to the selected hotel, resort, apartment, or villa without arranging transportation after landing.

Supported routes may include Puerto Plata, Playa Dorada, Sosúa, Cabarete, and other destinations in the region. Enter the exact accommodation or mapped location when searching. Availability and vehicle choices depend on the active route and total passenger count.

Puerto Plata and Playa Dorada

Provide the hotel or property name rather than only the city. This helps distinguish central Puerto Plata, the resort area, port connections, and surrounding accommodations.

Sosúa and Cabarete

For beach stays and water-sports travel, describe surfboards, kite equipment, or oversized luggage before confirming. The passenger total alone may not reflect the cargo space your party needs.

Return transportation to POP

Round-trip reservations should include the correct departure flight and date. Keep the confirmed pickup instructions available and be ready at the agreed meeting point, especially for villas or properties with controlled access.

Puerto Plata transfer questions

Can I bring surf or kite equipment?

Describe the number and dimensions of oversized items before booking. The team must confirm that the selected vehicle has suitable space.

Do you serve villas and apartments?

Use the exact mapped location and provide access or meeting instructions. Contact support if the destination does not appear in search.

Can I book transportation for a group?

Yes, subject to vehicle availability. Enter every passenger and all luggage needs, or contact the team for coordinated arrivals.
