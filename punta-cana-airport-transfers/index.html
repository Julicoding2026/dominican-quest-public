From PUJ arrivals to your accommodation

Punta Cana International Airport is the main arrival point for many vacations on the Dominican Republic’s east coast. A prearranged private transfer gives your party a clear plan after baggage claim and avoids having to organize a ride while navigating the arrival area.

Dominican Quest serves active routes between PUJ and hotels, resorts, villas, and communities throughout the region. Common destination areas include Punta Cana, Bávaro, Cap Cana, Cabeza de Toro, Arena Gorda, Macao, and Uvero Alto. Longer transfers to other regions can be searched through the booking form when an active route is available.

Meeting your airport driver

Your reservation should include the correct airline, flight number, arrival date, and passenger contact details. Follow the meeting instructions in your confirmation after leaving the terminal. Keep your phone available in case the dispatch team needs to coordinate with you.

Planning the right vehicle

Count every traveler in the party and include children when choosing the passenger total. Use the notes field for child seats, wheelchairs, golf equipment, surfboards, or unusually large luggage. Advance information helps the team determine whether the selected option is suitable.

Return transfers to PUJ

For a round trip, provide your departure-flight time rather than only the hotel checkout time. The booking flow can help schedule a return pickup with airport arrival time in mind. Confirm the final pickup details before travel day.

Punta Cana transfer questions

Can you pick up a group arriving on different flights?

For coordinated group travel, contact the reservations team with every flight and party breakdown. Separate pickups or a planned meeting arrangement may be needed.

Can I travel from PUJ to another part of the country?

Yes, when an active route and suitable vehicle are available. Enter the exact destination in the booking search or ask the team about a custom route.

How do I add a child seat request?

Include the request and child’s age in the special remarks before checkout so availability can be confirmed.
