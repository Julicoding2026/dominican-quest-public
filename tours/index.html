Choose an experience that fits your stay

Start with the location of your hotel or resort. A tour advertised from one region may require different transportation or may not include pickup from another area. The live booking section organizes available experiences by zone and shows the current tour type, description, duration, and price information supplied for that activity.

Regular and private tours

Regular tours are shared experiences operating according to the activity’s schedule and pickup plan. Private tours are designed for one party and may offer a more flexible group arrangement. Read the visible inclusions and pricing basis carefully before selecting.

Before confirming

  • Choose the correct resort zone and activity date.
  • Enter every adult and child according to the tour’s booking rules.
  • Review inclusions, what to bring, pickup details, and cancellation terms.
  • Tell the team about mobility considerations, dietary needs, or other important requirements.

Airport transfers and tour transportation

Tour pickup and airport transportation are different services. If your itinerary requires an airport ride, arrange it through the transfer booking form. For a custom multi-day plan or a large group, contact support with the complete itinerary.

Tour booking questions

How do I know whether pickup is available from my hotel?

Select the correct resort zone and read the activity details. If the property is outside the listed area, ask the reservations team before booking.

What is the difference between regular and private tours?

A regular tour is shared with other guests and follows the operator’s schedule. A private option is reserved for your party and uses the pricing and capacity shown for that activity.

Can I change the date after booking?

Contact support as early as possible. Date changes depend on the activity’s policy and remaining availability.
